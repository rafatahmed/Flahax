# Model limits are reported, never enforced as gates

Date: 2026-11-05 (decisions G1–G25 approved 2026-10-29)

FlahaX removes every gate that rests on a number or a condition nobody can guarantee: the 1 % and 0.1 % product charge limits, "EC not supported" above ionic strength 0.014, any positive saturation index blocking a recipe, stock "floors" that can never pass, the 5 % water imbalance rejection, and the zero-ammonium ban. Chemistry stays: it is computed from measured constants and declared analyses. Where the model reaches a limit, the limit is stated with the result (a stated model condition, an EC range, a precipitation risk label, a "needs input" status), and the nutrient recipe is still produced. Only a broken declared maximum blocks.

## Considered options

- **Keep the gates:** rejected. They made normal recipes fail, such as the potassium nitrate 13-0-46 label against pure KNO₃, and their thresholds had no source that could be defended.
- **Replace each gate with a new threshold:** rejected. A new number would be another unassurable value.

## Consequences

- A recipe can carry warnings and deviations; the grower reads them and decides. Reports must always show them.
- No new threshold, assumption, invented ion or midpoint may be introduced to restore a gate.
- Tests that asserted a refusal are rewritten to assert the report; no assertion is weakened.
