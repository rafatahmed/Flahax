# FlahaX 0.7.0: detailed notes

The full text of every change in 0.7.0, as recorded when each was merged, newest first. The [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md) groups them into a short summary. 0.7.0 is not yet released.

- The packaged catalogue is the owner's Flaha Agri Tech Analysis Ver 0.0 (Flaha Agri Tech, 2026-10-29; owner-supplied, not independent laboratory verification) (#206). `library.json` holds 29 products, 24 fertilizers and 5 pH products, 13 crop formulas and the 3 waters it held before. Every product carries the analysis review record (`analysisReview`), and the library records the analysis as a whole in `fertilizerAnalysis`. The pH products, all `role: ph_reagent` and never selected as nutrient salts, are Potassium Hydroxide (45% solution), Phosphoric Acid (75%), Nitric Acid (65% solution), Potassium Bicarbonate and Potassium Carbonate; `catalogue_ph_reagents()` returns all five, so `flahax batch` and `flahax mix` now evaluate the two packaged acids beside any acid given with `--acid`. New products: Nitric Acid (65% solution), Micro Combi Mix 1 and Micro Combi Mix 2. Removed products: Ammonium Dibasic Phosphate, Calcium Chloride (Dihydrate), CH - micro (kept as a retired reference in `retiredProductReferences`), Potassium Chloride, Potassium Citrate and Potassium Dibasic Phosphate; the packaged catalogue has no chloride product. Removed formulas: Chilli (maximumyield), Generic Bloom (maximumyield), Generic Dry Season (Howard Resh), Generic Grow (maximumyield), Generic Wet Season (Howard Resh), Generic for Berries (Growing Edge), Rice (Douglas Peckenpaugh) and Tropical Lettuce (Douglas Peckenpaugh). The owner's Potassium Nitrate analysis is 2.57 % out of charge balance against KNO3, above the 1 % label limit, so chemistry stages that include it stop with `identity_inconsistent`; that limit is under review. NOTICE and the attribution test credit Flaha Agri Tech for the analysis.
- Micro Combi Mix 1 and 2 have the owner's declared composition (owner decision 2026-10-30). Fe is Fe(III)-EDTA, Mn, Zn and Cu are EDTA chelates, Mo is sodium molybdate and, in Mix 2, B is boric acid. The sodium counter-ions follow the catalogue's own chelate identities (NaFeEDTA; Na2 Mn, Zn and Cu EDTA; Na2MoO4), and the ion forms are mol/kg from the owner's element percentages. The new identity status `declared_blend` resolves as a disclosed blend without the `assumed_composition` flag (`assumptionStatus`: `declared_composition_not_independently_verified`), and its chemistry source reads "owner-declared composition of" the product. Mix 2's chemistry profile names boric acid among its components (family `edta_boric_acid_molybdate_blend`), as CH-micro's profile names borax.
- Nitric acid is a strong acid in the engine's ion tables: `HNO3` dissociates fully into H+ and nitrate, the proton implicit, as for phosphoric acid. Nitric Acid (65% solution) converts to nitrate from its assay, with no counter-ions; parsing of the existing nitrate salts is unchanged.
- A `flahax batch` or `flahax mix` run whose formulation stage cannot meet a target, with no other stage blocking it, now ends "requires review" (exit 0) and reports the shortfall, for example K at -100 % when a K maximum of 0 forbids every potassium product (owner decision 2026-10-30). Before the owner catalogue such runs ended blocked (exit 3), because no packaged reagent could lower the pH; the packaged acids now give the pH stage a reagent.
- The engine-mechanics tests run on a frozen copy of the pre-owner catalogue (`tests/fixtures/catalogue-pre-owner-33c5df2.json`, the library at commit 33c5df2), entered through `tests.catalogue_history.historical_catalogue()`, so they keep testing the mechanics they were written for. Tests of the packaged catalogue itself run on the owner data.
