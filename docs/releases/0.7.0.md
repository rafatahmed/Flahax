## Problem and scope

Growers and agronomists need to turn crop targets, source-water analyses and
available products into a practical, reviewed batch recipe. This release adds
feed-target design and verified preparation to that workflow. It does not
operate equipment or decide season-long crop management.

Python 3.11 or newer; no runtime dependencies. The 135 package-level export
names from 0.6.0 remain and 16 names are added. This is not a guarantee of
unchanged signatures, statuses, defaults or saved-session compatibility.
Read the [migration guide](../site/start/migration.md) before reusing a workflow.

## Sonneveld feed design and feedback

Select the crop/system baseline, apply explicit nutrient corrections, and
adjust irrigation EC using Sonneveld's A-D sequence. Water credits and pH-reagent
nutrients enter the recipe calculation after target design. Design EC is the
paper's target-design convention; it is not the same calculation as the final
solution's modeled conductivity range.

Dated root-zone samples and guide values support a reviewed feed correction.
The grower chooses and approves the correction. A changed observation or design
requires a new approval; it does not silently change a previously saved recipe.
Feed targets and root-zone guide values describe different environments.

The [agronomist Appendix I guide](../site/agronomist/sonneveld-appendix-i.md)
reconstructs the article and explains the three calculation cases: the fixed
article acid dose, a pH 5.5 recipe with the restricted inventory, and a pH 5.5
fit using sodium nitrate with its sodium contribution disclosed. Later regressions
extend this to a broader eligible inventory at pH 5.5 without sodium nitrate. Source water, product forms
and actual reagent nutrients are counted. The
[grower walkthrough](../site/grower/sonneveld-walkthrough.md) shows the review path.
These are illustrative inputs, not laboratory analyses or crop recommendations.

## Recipe search and practical preparation

The bounded product search evaluates broader eligible combinations when the
first recipe misses. It retains verified candidates when later attempts fail,
ranks tolerance attainment before declared preferences and reports the best
result found within the evaluated budget. It does not claim global optimality.
Only explicitly available/eligible products may be used; sodium nitrate remains
excluded in the worked alternative-inventory case.

Volumetric product stocks make trace doses such as Appendix I copper practical.
Batch and TUI preparation count rounded dry grams, stock mL and reagent doses,
including carrier water. Stock carrier displaces source water rather than
adding an uncounted extra volume. Product composition, stock concentration,
dispensing and physical bounds follow the declared inputs.

Verification reconstructs the delivered nutrients after rounding and evaluates
the supplied dry-fertilizer/reagent uncertainty, nutrient ceilings and reserve.
The worked example uses a 5% sodium reserve; this is an explicit example setting,
not a universal crop-safety rule. pH intervals and sampled saturation screens
retain their stated domain and failure evidence. A sampled range is not a proof
over every continuous uncertainty combination or mixing sequence.

Stock assignment, single-product solubility and mixed-stock compatibility are
distinct checks. Missing preparation evidence remains visible. Real product
assays, scale/dispenser calibration, mixed concentrates and finished-feed
measurements still need physical qualification.

## Current numerical and reporting contracts

- Nitrate-N, ammonium-N and urea-N are distinct fitting targets; total N is their
  reported sum. Missing urea is zero. Missing/zero ammonium is no requirement,
  with the documented default allowance. Explicit maxima remain constraints.
- Positive target tolerance is 1e-5 relative; declared-zero tolerance is 1e-6 mg/L,
  except the documented ammonium semantics; numerical pH tolerance is 1e-4.
  Rounded-preparation tolerances are supplied separately. None is an agronomic
  suitability threshold.
- Nutrient loss excludes hidden dose and incidental-ion penalties. Nonzero ridge
  is unsupported. Local stationarity, target matching, declared-limit compliance
  and preparation status are reported separately.
- Water is retained as measured with residual charge and explicit carbon/solvent
  assumptions. Supported label reconciliation discloses unassigned assay amounts;
  it does not invent their chemical identities.
- EC reports estimated or range, without a midpoint. The disclosed upper-estimate
  convention is not a proven physical bound. Saturation evidence reports risk or
  information and does not predict precipitation rates.
- Selected reagent availability is explicit. Returned reagent quantities determine
  final nutrient accounting, including sequential fallback. Reagent containers can
  be refilled independently; solid reagents are reported by mass.

## Profiles, sessions and compatibility

`flahax mix --sonneveld-batch FILE` imports the documented batch profile for
preview and review. Saved sessions embed the input needed for repeat; they bind
source/input and selected-candidate identity. Repeating a session after deleting
its original import file is supported. Changes require recalculation and review,
not editing a saved acceptance into validity. Legacy sessions that lack reagent
availability need explicit reagent review and a new session name; other source
or input drift checks still apply.

The deterministic replay correction orders analytical components consistently
before numerical evaluation. TUI verification retains the rounded preparation
checks and reports. The 13 real catalogue formula regressions are independently
scheduled without increasing their timeout or weakening their assertions.

See [file/session contracts](../site/reference/file-formats.md) for exact schemas
and units. Package version, input schema, model/source identity, observation
approval and result acceptance are different identifiers.

Removed contracts include water balance-ion/completeness controls, retired EC
partial/refusal statuses and phase blocks fields. Five University of Florida
tomato presets and extension-based pH/bicarbonate advice are no longer supplied.
User-saved targets remain user inputs. Recalculate and review rather than
silently substituting new targets or transferring an old acceptance.
