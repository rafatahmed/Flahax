# FlahaX 0.7.1 — 2026-11-28

**Declared artifact release date: 2026-11-28.** This declaration does not assert that a registry
upload, release tag or version-specific archive exists. The tag commit, GitHub Release, PyPI
files, version DOI and CI runs are recorded after publication, in the
[publication evidence](../site/evidence/release-evidence.md). The
[0.7.0 notes](0.7.0.md) describe the previous published release.

## Problem and scope

0.7.0 let growers and agronomists turn crop targets, source-water analyses and available
products into a reviewed batch recipe. 0.7.1 keeps that scope and tightens how it is used:
what a recipe may assume about ammonium, how total nitrogen is entered, what a report says it
does and does not establish, what equipment a recipe must fit, and how the library fails when an
input is wrong. It adds no crop model and does not operate equipment. Python 3.11 or newer; no
runtime dependencies.

Read the [migration guide](../site/start/migration.md#from-070-to-071) before reusing a
0.7.0 workflow: default recipes can differ (see below), and some internal names moved.

## What changes for a grower or agronomist

- **Ammonium is no longer assumed.** An absent or zero `N_NH4` target is no requirement and no
  maximum. The 10% allowance (the smaller of 10% of total N and 1.5 mmol/L) is now an opt-in
  preset, `max_ammonium_fraction=0.10`. A default recipe may therefore use ammonium nitrate and
  reports the NH4-N it brings, for review. This is the one change that can alter a recipe you
  calculated with 0.7.0.
- **Total nitrogen is a target.** `N` (mg/L as N) can be given alone, with nitrogen forms, or
  with a declared nitrate and ammonium: with both it must equal their sum (an absent urea counts
  as zero), and an inconsistent total is refused before fitting.
- **Acceptable ranges and pH intervals.** `--range-percent`, `--range` and `--require-ranges`
  (Python: `deviation_ranges`) classify, or constrain, how far a nutrient may deviate; none is
  applied unless you give one. `target_ph` accepts an interval (`--ph-min`, `--ph-max`): a mix
  already inside needs no reagent, otherwise the plan aims at the nearest bound.
- **Equipment fit.** `flahax batch --available-tanks N --tank-capacity LITRES` lists the tanks
  the recipe needs against yours, and a tank too small for its stock blocks the batch without
  changing the recipe. `--fit-equipment` searches other products for a recipe that fits the
  declared tanks; tanks and separation rules are never changed.
- **Solubility is qualified.** A solubility value is `verified` only where the recorded
  temperature range and concentration basis support it. With no limit at the tank temperature
  the quantities are kept, the product is listed as unverified and nothing is approved (this
  replaces the `missing_solubility_limit` error).
- **Reports say less and more.** Every result states that no real-mixture predictive accuracy is
  claimed; dissolved species and any predicted precipitate are listed apart from the nutrient
  totals, with the statement that meeting a target does not show nutrients stay dissolved or
  available; a met target is described by its numeric criterion (1e-05 relative, 1e-06 mg/L for
  a declared zero). `docs/physical-validation-protocol.md` drafts the staged physical validation
  that [issue #41](https://github.com/rafatahmed/Flahax/issues/41) asks to have reviewed; it
  authorizes no laboratory work.
- **Saved results explain recalculation.** A saved result keeps the inputs it was calculated
  from, bound to its fingerprint, and a recalculation names what changed.

## What changes for a developer

- **Clearer failures.** A solver that gives up names its stage, iteration count and residual in
  the message, and `DeliveryError.details` carries the same facts (including the earlier
  warm-start and cold-start failures of `solve`). Messages keep their wording and gain a bracketed
  suffix; code that matches a whole message must match its start.
- **Stricter inputs.** A measured concentration above 2,000,000 mg/L (more than a litre of
  solution can hold) or a boolean is an `InputError` instead of an overflow; a maximum is an upper
  bound and still accepts any finite value. A water file with a NaN or infinite `measured_ph` is
  refused. `solve` and `nitric_target` raise `DeliveryError` for a pH, total or ionic strength
  that is not a number, instead of `TypeError`. Numeric strings are no longer coerced to numbers
  in delivery contracts. Products sharing an exact name are rejected as `ambiguous_product_name`.
- **Composed plans are checked field by field.** `compose_delivery_plan`, `delivery_report` and
  `transition_plan` refuse a pH plan, pump command or stock plan whose own fields contradict each
  other (`cross_stage_constraint`). A plan altered consistently cannot be detected, because stage
  values do not keep their source records.
- **Moved names.** Sixteen helpers lost their leading underscore; the nitrogen rules live in
  `flahax.nitrogen`; `run_batch` and `BatchOptions` are in `flahax.batch_request`; the water
  reader is in `flahax.water_input`; `grower_report` is split into `batch_summary`,
  `report_html`, `result_wording` and `report_format`. Names exported from `flahax`, commands and
  outputs are unchanged. `validate_targets` is the one targets check; `validate_profile` no longer
  takes the `total_n` flag.
- **Same digits everywhere.** Float sums give identical digits on Python 3.11 and 3.12 or newer
  (3.12 changed the built-in `sum`), and a test compares recorded digits on every CI leg.

## Speed

`import flahax` loads no submodule; the first `recommend` call takes about 67 ms instead of 90 ms;
the aqueous solve is faster with bit-identical results, and each pH point of the uncertainty grid
starts from its nearest solved pH, which changes results only within the solver tolerance. The
full test suite no longer takes about an hour. `python -m tools.performance_budgets check` compares
import, first-call and solver work with agreed budgets: counts fail the check, timings relative to
a calibration loop are reported. Timings were measured on one Windows machine and are not a
performance claim.

## Release process

The release workflow installs `build`, `twine` and setuptools from
`tools/release/requirements-release.txt` with `--require-hashes` and builds without isolation, so the
artifacts are made by reviewed tool versions; Dependabot proposes monthly updates.

## Corrections

Total nitrogen counts an absent urea as zero; coupled recipes are assessed at the emitted reagent
dose and keep their search provenance; the workflow accepts only its five documented stage
statuses; `tools/check_numeric_review.py` reports classification and scientific qualification as
separate outcomes; the glossary no longer implies a fixed A/B pair of tanks.

## Limits that remain

No independent physical validation exists ([issue #41](https://github.com/rafatahmed/Flahax/issues/41));
this remains a planning and verification prototype, not dosing certification. The `measured_ph` of a
water record and the starting pH of its titration curve are not compared. Pump calibration,
product assays, mixed concentrates and finished-feed measurements still need physical
qualification.
